MY MUSIC PLAYLIST

My Love for Music

Music is one of my favorite things because it brings happiness, peace, and positive energy into my life. I enjoy listening to different kinds of music depending on my mood. Sometimes I listen to energetic songs when I want to feel motivated, while calm and relaxing music helps me feel peaceful. Music is also a great way to express emotions and connect with memories. A particular song can remind me of a special moment, place, or person. I enjoy discovering new songs and artists and exploring different styles of music. Whenever I have free time, I like to listen to music while relaxing, travelling, or doing my daily activities. Music helps me take a break from stress and makes ordinary moments more enjoyable. For me, music is not just something to listen to—it is a source of inspiration, comfort, and happiness that adds a special feeling to everyday life.

MY FAVOURITE ARTIST

My favourite artist is Sid Sriram

I like Sid Sriram because of his unique and soulful voice. His songs are full of emotion and have a special way of connecting with listeners. I enjoy the way he expresses feelings through his singing, making every song feel meaningful. His voice is calming, powerful, and memorable, which is why he is one of my favorite singers.
